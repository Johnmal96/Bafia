Read the ring, know the job.

Every job we take runs through the same core disciplines — from the subfloor up to the final detail on doors, cabinets, and custom woodwork.

  1. 01

    Hardwood floors

    New installs and full refinishing — sanding, staining, and sealing existing floors back to their original grain.

  2. 02

    Stairs

    Treads, risers, and railings built and fitted to match the rest of the home's woodwork.

  3. 03

    Doors & cabinets

    Hanging, fitting, and installation for doors, cabinets, and built-in storage that look right and function well.

  4. 04

    Trim & millwork

    Baseboards, casing, crown molding, and other finished details cut and coped for a tight, clean fit.

  5. 05

    Custom woodwork

    Built-to-spec pieces, repairs, and special projects that don't fit a category — bring the job, we'll bring the fix.
